Saki Nishioka
Pianist

Born in Nara, Japan, Saki Nishioka studied at Shitennoji High School before graduating with a degree in Piano Performance from the Kyoto City University of Arts. She then moved to Paris to continue her studies at the Conservatoire National Supérieur de Musique et de Danse de Paris (CNSMDP), where she was admitted unanimously with the highest distinction, and graduated with the highest honours in 2018.

As a scholarship student of the Kyoto French Music Academy, she simultaneously studied at the École Normale de Musique de Paris, earning the Diplôme de Concertiste. She was also a scholarship recipient of the Aoyama Music Foundation in 2013 and 2014.

Her competition achievements include 2nd Prize at the 61st All-Japan Student Music Competition (National Finals), 1st Prize at the 19th Hüpfeld Tosu Piano Competition, the Encouragement Prize at the 18th Matsukata Hall Music Award, and 2nd Prize & Audience Award at the 5th International Fauré Piano Competition (France), among numerous other prizes including finals and distinctions at the PTNA Piano Competition.

Nishioka performs solo recitals across Japan and Europe and has appeared as a concerto soloist with orchestras internationally, including the Ribeirão Preto Symphony Orchestra in Brazil. In France, she has been invited to prestigious festivals such as the Paris Chopin Festival, Lisztomanias, Piano en Valois, and Piano aux Jacobins.

She has studied under Claudio Soares, Hiroyuki Abe, Bruno Rigutto, Hortense Cartier-Bresson, and Fernando Rossano.

In 2022, she appeared on NHK-FM's Récital Passio. In 2023, she was selected for the prestigious Phoenix Evolution Series at the Aioi Nissay Dowa Insurance The Phoenix Hall (Osaka), where her recital devoted to Fauré's complete Nocturnes and Impromptus was met with high acclaim.

Between October 2023 and April 2024, she recorded the complete Fauré piano solo works — equivalent to four CDs, including transcriptions — in just ten days. The recordings were released in 2025 in two volumes.

In February 2026, invited by Musashino City Cultural Hall in Tokyo, she performed an ambitious seven-hour programme of Fauré: The Complete Piano Works to great critical acclaim. Also in 2026, she joined as a collaborative pianist on the Japan tour of Tamás Varga, Principal Cellist of the Vienna Philharmonic Orchestra.

She currently holds a position as Part-time Lecturer at Kyoto City University of Arts.
